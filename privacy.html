Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 6, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Summarizer & Translator AI. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and related services (collectively, the "Service").

By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please do not use our Service.

2. Information We Collect

We collect several types of information to provide and improve our Service:

a) Account Information
When you create an account, we collect your email address and an encrypted password.

b) Usage Data
We automatically collect certain information including device type, OS version, session duration, and features accessed.

c) Content Data
Text, documents, audio, or images you submit are processed by our AI service providers. This content is transmitted securely and is not permanently stored after processing.

d) Language Preferences
We store your preferred language settings locally on your device.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Service
  • Process your summarization and translation requests
  • Manage your account and provide customer support
  • Send service-related notifications (with your consent)
  • Monitor usage patterns and improve the Service
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical issues

We do not use your content data for training AI models.

4. Data Retention

We follow a strict zero-data-retention policy for content processing:

  • Content submitted for processing is not stored after the response is delivered.
  • Your history is stored locally on your device and can be cleared at any time.
  • Account data is retained as long as your account is active.
  • If you delete your account, all associated data will be permanently removed within 30 days.

5. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share data with:

a) AI Processing Providers — OpenAI's API for content processing

b) Authentication Services — Supabase for user authentication

c) Analytics — Anonymized usage analytics

d) Legal Requirements — When required by law

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • TLS 1.3 encryption for all data in transit
  • Hashed passwords using bcrypt
  • Secure API key storage
  • Regular security audits
  • Multi-factor authentication for production access

7. Your Rights

You may have the right to:

  • Access and export your personal data
  • Update your account information
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Opt-out of notifications
  • Object to certain data processing activities

8. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your country of residence. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place.
